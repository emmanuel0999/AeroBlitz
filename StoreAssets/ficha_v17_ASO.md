--- App name (max 30) ---
Aero Blitz: Space Shooter

--- Short description (max 80) ---
Space shooter: beat 7 epic bosses, collect 11 ships and conquer 70 levels

--- Full description (max 4000) ---
Think you can beat 7 epic bosses and all 70 levels? Aero Blitz is a fast arcade space shooter where every shot counts. Charge your weapon, dodge asteroids, drones, mines and laser gates, face giant bosses and fly across 10 galaxy worlds to the top of the world leaderboard.

NEW: 7 BOSS BATTLES, THE GALACTIC ROUTE AND SHARPER GRAPHICS

7 EPIC BOSSES
• At levels 10, 20, 30... 70 a giant boss blocks your way: Mechanical Destroyer, Combat Spider, Intergalactic Battleship, Ghost Ship, Hive Queen, Supreme AI and Galactic Emperor.
• Every boss has 3 phases. Learn its attack patterns, shoot down the drones and mines it summons, and survive its fury mode when its health runs low.
• Beat a boss for the first time to win bonus coins and a rare chest.
• Skipped a boss on an earlier version? Challenge it from the Galactic Route whenever you want.

THE GALACTIC ROUTE
See your whole journey across the 10 worlds: where you are, which worlds you have cleared and which boss is waiting ahead.

TIME YOUR SHOTS
Your weapon does not fire forever. Fill the charge bar, unleash a burst of shots, then survive while it recharges. Picking the right moment is what separates a great pilot from a wreck.

11 SPACESHIPS TO COLLECT
• 8 ships you unlock with coins earned in the game, from the Falcon to the Specter.
• 3 premium ships with a metallic finish, a passive ability and their own ability button: Phoenix (Rebirth + Flare), Titan (Bulwark + Total Shield) and Nova (Piercing + Overdrive).
• Give any ship a Cartoon or Neon style, new paints and engine trails, all with coins.

A REASON TO COME BACK EVERY DAY
• 3 daily missions and a weekly mission that rewards exclusive paints.
• 7-day streak: coins, a chest, an exclusive paint and a premium ship to try for 24 hours.
• Common, rare and epic chests, earned only by playing and never sold.
• 12 achievements worth 13,600 coins.

NOT EVERYTHING CAN BE DESTROYED
Blast asteroids and drones, but watch out for walls, mines and lasers: your shots can't break them, and crashing into them takes a big chunk of your health.

WEAPONS THAT BREAK THROUGH
Earn stronger cannons and fire homing missiles with their own button: they destroy the walls and mines your shots can't touch, and they hit bosses hard. From level 25, trial weapons can drop during a run.

COINS AND POWER-UPS
From level 16, and in every boss fight, grab coins and power-ups mid-flight: Shield, Overcharge, Magnet and Repair. Collected coins only count if you clear the level!

10 WORLDS, 70 LEVELS
Fly from the Outer Belt to the Galactic Core through ice rings, crimson nebulas, solar flares and plasma seas. Every world has its own look and music, and the difficulty keeps rising until the final levels.

UPGRADE YOUR SPACESHIP
Upgrade armor, engine, magazine and capacitor, and equip extra gear to survive the hardest levels and the toughest bosses.

CLIMB THE LEADERBOARD
Pick your pilot name and country, then compete on the world leaderboard and your country's leaderboard. Can you reach the top 50?

MORE FLYING, LESS WAITING
You have 5 lives. If you lose them all, they fully refill in 4 hours, and every video you watch gives you 1 extra life (up to 3 every 4 hours).

PLAY ANYWHERE
The campaign works offline. The leaderboard, videos and cloud backup need a connection. Sign in with Google Play Games (optional) to back up your progress and restore it on a new phone.

Aero Blitz is free to play. It contains ads and optional in-app purchases (coins, lives, premium ships, equipment, remove ads and a VIP subscription). No purchase is needed to finish the game.

ARMAS PARA ABRIRTE PASO
Consigue cañones más potentes y lanza misiles teledirigidos con su propio botón: destrozan muros y minas que tus disparos no pueden tocar, y pegan fuerte a los jefes. Desde el nivel 25 pueden caer armas de prueba durante la partida.

Desde el nivel 16, y en todos los combates contra jefes, recoge monedas y mejoras en pleno vuelo: Escudo, Sobrecarga, Imán y Reparación. ¡Las monedas recogidas solo cuentan si superas el nivel!

Mejora el blindaje, el motor, el cargador y el condensador, y equipa accesorios para sobrevivir a los niveles más difíciles y a los jefes más duros.
