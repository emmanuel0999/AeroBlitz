--- Full description (max 4000) ---
Aero Blitz is a fast arcade space shooter where every shot counts. Pilot your spaceship through 70 levels across 10 worlds packed with asteroids, drones, mines and laser gates, and decide in a split second whether to dodge or fire.

TIME YOUR SHOTS
Your weapon does not fire forever. Fill the charge bar, unleash a burst of shots for a few seconds, then survive while it recharges. Picking the right moment to shoot is what separates a great pilot from a wreck.

NOT EVERYTHING CAN BE DESTROYED
Blast asteroids and drones, but watch out for walls, mines and lasers: your shots can't break them, and crashing into them takes away a big chunk of your ship's health.

WEAPONS THAT BREAK THROUGH
Earn stronger cannons and fire homing missiles with their own button: they destroy the walls and mines your shots can't touch. From level 25, trial weapons can drop during a run.

COINS AND POWER-UPS
From level 16, grab coins and temporary power-ups mid-flight: Shield, Overcharge, Magnet and Repair. Collected coins only count if you clear the level!

10 WORLDS, 70 LEVELS
Fly from the Outer Belt to the Galactic Core through ice rings, crimson nebulas, solar flares and plasma seas. Every world has its own look and its own music, and the difficulty keeps rising until the final levels.

UPGRADE YOUR SPACESHIP
• Unlock new ships, each with its own stats.
• Upgrade armor, engine, magazine and capacitor.
• Equip extra gear and paint your ship.

CLIMB THE LEADERBOARD
Pick your pilot name and country, then compete on the world leaderboard and your country's leaderboard. Can you reach the top 50?

YOUR PROGRESS IS SAFE
Sign in with Google Play Games (optional) to back up your progress and restore it on a new phone.

FEATURES
• Arcade shoot 'em up action with vertical scrolling.
• Unique charge-and-fire weapon system.
• 70 levels in 10 worlds, each with its own soundtrack.
• Ships, upgrades and equipment.
• World and country leaderboards.
• English, Spanish and French.
• 3 lives that refill over time.

Aero Blitz is free to play. It contains ads and optional in-app purchases (coins, lives, ships, equipment, remove ads and a VIP subscription). No purchase is needed to finish the game.
