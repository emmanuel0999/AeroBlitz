Aero Blitz is an arcade space shooter where every shot counts. Battle epic bosses, unlock powerful spaceships, upgrade your weapons and conquer 70 action-packed levels across the galaxy. Play online or offline and climb the global leaderboard in this fast-paced sci-fi shooter.

NEW: 7 BOSS BATTLES, THE GALACTIC ROUTE AND SHARPER GRAPHICS

7 EPIC BOSS BATTLES
• Face a giant boss at levels 10, 20, 30, 40, 50, 60 and 70.
• Battle the Mechanical Destroyer, Combat Spider, Intergalactic Battleship, Ghost Ship, Hive Queen, Supreme AI and Galactic Emperor.
• Every boss features 3 combat phases, unique attack patterns and a powerful fury mode.
• Defeat a boss for the first time to earn bonus coins and a rare chest.
• Challenge previously missed bosses anytime from the Galactic Route.

THE GALACTIC ROUTE
• Follow your journey through 10 galaxy worlds.
• Track your progress and discover which boss awaits ahead.

UNIQUE SHOOTING MECHANICS
This isn't a typical shooter game. Your weapon uses a charge system.
• Fill your charge bar.
• Release a burst of powerful shots.
• Survive while your weapon recharges.
• Master timing and strategy to become an elite pilot.
